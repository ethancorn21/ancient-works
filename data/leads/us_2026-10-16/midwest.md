# Midwest / Ohio Valley / Great Lakes plan sources (OH KY WV IN IL WI MN IA MI PA NY)

Rows: `midwest.jsonl` (23 rows, 17 sites; 21 verified, 2 likely). Source rows: `midwest_rows/*.json`, rebuilt by `midwest_bin/build.py`.
Rows in `docs/lead_us_mounds.md` are not repeated. Leaves are IA `/page/nNNN` and every one was checked against a page image (thumbnail or larger).
Licence: everything is pre-1931 or a US federal publication. The Hopewell survey row cites the 1926 OAHQ first printing; the Shetrone 1930 book reprint is listed only as an alternate image.

## Sites with new finds

- **hopewell-mound-group** - (1) Shetrone 1926, OAHQ 35(1) (sim_ohio-history_1926-01_35_1), fold-out map split over n5 and n6. This is F. R. Jones's 1922-25 instrument survey, with the road, the railroad, a legend separating existing from obliterated works, and a 0-1000 ft scale. It is the best Hopewell drawing found. The same survey appears on one page in Shetrone 1930 Fig. 120 (moundbuildersrec00shet n229). (2) Atwater 1820 Pl. VI, n205, the earliest plan.
- **seip-earthworks** - Atwater 1820 Pl. VII, n161 (with Baum and the Spruce Hill fort). S&D Pl. XXI No. 2, n142 ("estate of John Woodbridge", 5 mi above Baum). Shetrone fig. 168 (n295) is the Pricer-works plan that is already in the lead (Fowke fig. 41), so no row.
- **frankfort-works** - S&D Pl. XXI No. 4, n142.
- **hopewell-culture-NHP (Mound City point)** - S&D Pl. XIX, n136, and Fowke fig. 35, n222. Shetrone fig. 130 (n240) is redrawn from S&D, so no row.
- **mound-cemetery (and the marietta overlay)** - Atwater 1820 Pl. IV, n148 (Putnam survey). MacLean 1903 OAHQ 12, four plans: Heart 1787 (n52), Putnam (n58), DeWitt (n72), and Whittlesey 1837 drawn over the town street grid (n73, plus the supplementary plan at n76). The Whittlesey plan is the most fit-friendly.
- **horseshoe-mound** - Atwater 1820 Pl. VIII, n170. All the Portsmouth works on both banks.
- **piketon-mounds** - Atwater 1820 Pl. XI, n220.
- **newark** - Atwater 1820 Pl. II, n134. The earliest published Newark plan: lettered key, scale of chains, and Newark village drawn in.
- **fort-ancient** - Atwater 1820 Pl. IX, n176. Shows the state road from Chillicothe to Lebanon, Upper, Middle and Lower forts, outlying mounds and parallel walls.
- **fudge** - Dunn 1919, p.11, n62. Cox's 1878 Indiana Geological Survey plan from G. M. Levette's measurements. Wall bearings and lengths, the PUBLIC ROAD and NORTH LINE OF FAIR GROUND lines, the mound, and the west gateway screen. Cox says the S&D plat (which the current overlay uses) "was inaccurately made". This is the strongest new overlay source.
- **tremper-mound-and-works** - Mills 1916 OAHQ 25 Fig. 2, n278. Contoured topographic survey by Mobbs, 1915.
- **halvorson-mound-group** - Brown 1912, Wisconsin Archeologist o.s. 11(1), Plate 8 "Yahara Group No. 2", n48. Text: "Halverson farm", S1/2 Sec. 23, Westport; BLM PLSS confirms Sec. 23, T8N R9E.
- **blackhawk-country-club** - Peet 1890, Prehistoric America vol. 2, fig. 167 "Group at Merrill's Springs", n298. Rated *likely*: the springs group is about 800 m N of the club point, and whether it reaches the club tract is not proven. Stout's 1905 plat of the 12-mound club group is cited only in 1940s volumes.
- **mount-horeb** - Young 1910, p.51, n74, "Earthwork - Fayette County": Newtown Pike, North Fork Elkhorn, moat and plateau. Rated *likely* because the identification rests on the text location.
- **mounds-state-park** - Dunn 1919 Plate E "Earth Mounds Near Anderson", n77. White River, scale 1 in = 250 ft; text: "group of eight, shown on plate E". The original is probably Cox 1878, with a second account in Walker 1892 (Proc. Ind. Acad. Sci. p.51). Neither was checked.
- **emerald-mound-and-village (IL)** - Snyder 1909, J. Ill. State Hist. Soc. vol. 2, Fig. 2 "Diagrams", n77: mound M, terrace T, spring, brook, mounds 1-4. It is a sketch diagram. Snyder's 1894 "An Illinois Teocalli" (The Archaeologist, Sept. 1894) is a further lead that was not checked.
- **grave-creek-mound** - Schoolcraft 1851 vol. 1 Pl. 39 "Map of Grave Creek Flats, Virginia", n195 (federal and 1851). Also in the 1860 reprint, archivesaborknow01schorich.

## Searched, nothing new usable

- **aberdeen-mound** - Thomas fig. 311 is the Hill place, 4 mi N of Ripley, so it does not match. FTS found nothing else pre-1931.
- **alligator-effigy-mound** - Pipeline already has S&D Pl. XXXVI and a Commons 1891 image. Shetrone fig. 149 (n270) is a redraw, so no row.
- **angel-mounds** - Pipeline already has Thomas Pl. XLI and fig. 339. Shetrone fig. 262 (n451) is "after BAE", so no row.
- **arledge, beam-farm, norwood, shrum, zaleski, hodgen's, sinnissippi, kincaid, wickliffe** - IA FTS found no pre-1931 plan. Wickliffe has text only, in Young 1910. Funkhouser & Webb 1928 (kgs6ri341928crs) has no site plans and its images are redacted.
- **aztalan** - Pipeline has Lapham 1855 Pl. XXXIV-XXXV. Peet 1890 Diagram VII (prehistoricameri02peetuoft, p.209) and Wisconsin Archeologist 1901 Plate 10 are Lapham-derived. Hyer's 1837 first map (Milwaukee Advertiser) was not found in any pre-1931 reprint on IA.
- **biggs-site** - Already in the lead (Fowke figs. 19-20, S&D Pl. XXVIII). Fowke fig. 20 (n201) reproduces the S&D *view* (fig. 19), not a plan, so check it before fitting. Atwater Pl. VIII shows the KY "Old Fort" (Group A), about 5 km W of Biggs, so it does not cover Biggs.
- **cahokia** - Pipeline already has Thomas Pl. VI, S&D fig. 60 and Patrick 1876 (Commons/DPLA). Moorehead 1922 (cahokiamoundswit0000moor) has no new site map in its text. McAdams 1887 (recordsofancient00mcad) has none either.
- **criel-mound** - Already in the lead (Thomas Pl. XXVII). Shetrone fig. 155 (n278) is Thomas-derived.
- **forthill, highbank, hopeton** - Nothing found beyond S&D, Fowke, Mills and Shetrone redraws: Shetrone fig. 113 High Bank (n219), fig. 144 Fort Hill, fig. 165 Hopeton.
- **grand-mound (MN)** - Bryce 1885 is text only. Winchell 1911 *Aborigines of Minnesota* (HathiTrust coo.31924028652661, full view) was blocked by Cloudflare. It is an unresolved lead.
- **indian-mound-reserve** - S&D XXXIV No. 3 (the Massie's Creek group half a mile below the fort) is not confirmed to lie inside the reserve. The lead already has S&D and Fowke fig. 52.
- **indian-mounds-park (St Paul)** - Hill 1894 (MHS Coll. vol. 6) is text only; the map is referenced but not printed. The St Paul park report p.58 on Commons is an accounts table. Winchell 1911 was blocked (see grand-mound).
- **lizard-mound** - This is the Hagner group (Sec. 32 T12N R20E). Lapham Pl. XI (n136) is the adjacent Sec. 31 group, so it does not match. Torney's 1883 sketch was never published pre-1931. Freckmann 1942 is not PD.
- **miamisburg-mound** - Only views and photos: S&D fig. 1, Fowke fig. 91, OAHQ 14 p.446, Shetrone fig. 100. A conical mound needs no plan anyway.
- **odd-fellows-cemetery-mound** - Whittlesey's "Mound near Newtown" (1 mi N, 40 ft high) does not match the site (oval 110x90 ft, 11 ft high).
- **pyramid-mound (IN)** - The 1873 Indiana Geological Survey report (annualreportgeo04geolgoog p.373) has only a view, and that plate is missing from the Google scan.
- **serpent-mound** - Already heavily covered: 26 entries in plans.json (S&D, Thomas/Holmes, Putnam via Fowke, Commons). Nothing added.
- **story-mound** - OAHQ 7 (1898) has a photo and an excavation post plan only.
- **Fort Ancient extras** - Moorehead 1890 (b24881740):
  - Plate V, Sheppard's map (n43), copied from S&D according to Moorehead. No row.
  - Plate 2, the Fowke & Cowen 1888 survey. Its labels are in the OCR (hocr idx 46), but it was not located on a leaf image (n44-46 look like text or blank pages). Probably the same survey as Fowke 1902 fig. 59, which is already in the pipeline.
  - An appendix of station bearings and distances for every section of embankment, i.e. a full traverse that could be digitised.
  - The 1908 edition (fortancientgreat0000moor) has a "Map of Fort Ancient (Survey of 1889)".
  - Randall 1908/1916 *Masterpieces* has a "Plan of Fort Ancient".
  - None of these was checked page by page.
- **Books checked with no target-site plans** - MacLean 1879 *The Mound Builders* (moundbuildersbei00macl): the illustrations are S&D-style redraws of Fort Ancient, Newark, Marietta and Piketon, plus Butler County works, none of which are on the site list. Moorehead 1892 *Primitive Man in Ohio* (primitivemanohio00moorrich): Hopewell figures are a mound ground plan and artefacts, with no group plan. Peet 1890 vol. 2 and McAdams 1887: see the site entries above.
- **Newark extra lead** - Wyrick 1860 map in Beers 1866 *Atlas of Licking County*. It is not on IA, and LoC was blocked (403). Not verified.

## Blocked during the search

HathiTrust (Cloudflare), LoC (403) and Google Books API (quota) were all blocked, and the Chrome extension was not connected. The Winchell 1911 Minnesota volume and the Beers 1866 atlas are the main leads left behind these blocks.
