# Lead: Numancia — the excavation plans of the 1860s (task 210)

**Status: VERIFIED — the source exists, is free and machine-readable.** Decision: PROCEED.
Verified 2026-10-12 (sessions 1-3 of task 210; plate eye-confirmed from the scan this session).

The 1860s excavation plan of Numancia is **LÁMINA IV, fig. 5, "Plano de las excavaciones
hechas en Numancia en 1855"** of Eduardo Saavedra's *Descripción de la vía romana de Úxama
á Augustóbriga*, as published in *Memorias de la Real Academia de la Historia*, tomo IX
(Madrid, **1879**). It is the excavation-era plan the lead asks for: a plan of the
excavation trenches on the cerro de Garray (the site the 1853-66 campaigns worked),
drawn by the excavation's own director.

## 1. The source and its machine-readable copy (verified)

**The work.** Eduardo Saavedra (ingeniero de caminos), *Descripción de la vía romana de
Úxama á Augustóbriga* — prize essay written 1858, awarded 26 Apr 1861 (Saavedra elected
académico 20 Dec 1861). Printed as the third memoir of RAH *Memorias* tomo IX (1879); the
volume's Noticia histórica notes it "debiera en rigor haber sido impresa aparte, como
premiada en el concurso de 1861", and the 1877 Boletín footnote records "Está impresa la
Descripción, 112 págs. Índices, cuatro hojas, 8 págs. sin número, **con cinco láminas**,
en el tomo IX de las Memorias de la Academia". The 1879-edition title per Gómez Gonzalo
(Revista de Soria 100): "Descripción de la Vía Romana entre Garray y Renieblas".

**The machine-readable copy (free).** Internet Archive item **`memoriasdelareal009real`**
(https://archive.org/details/memoriasdelareal009real): RAH *Memorias de la Real Academia

creator Real Academia de la Historia (Spain), publisher Madrid; scanned 2012 by Boston

## 5. Who actually dug (the GOAL's "Villanueva" attribution)

Chronology (Gómez Gonzalo, Revista de Soria 100, plus the 1877 Boletín report):
1842 first (municipal) digs · **1853 Saavedra's explorations on the Cerro de la Muela
(fixed the site — the campaign the staged plan depicts)** · 1858 prize essay to the RAH,
awarded 26 Apr 1861, Saavedra elected académico 20 Dec 1861 · **1861-66 the first
government-funded (RAH Comisión) excavations** (the 1877 Boletín's Garray report: began
12 Aug 1861, ~10,000 reales) · 1877 Boletín tomo I prints that report — **text-only**
("…en la confianza de llegar á descubrir el plano") · 1879 Saavedra's work published
(tomo IX). Later decades (not this lead's subject): 1905-12 Schulten's German
expedition; 1906-35 Mélida's official Spanish excavation. The GOAL's "Villanueva" is a
mix-up with the 18th-c. architect Juan de Dios Villanueva; the 1860s campaign belonged
to Saavedra (and, per the Cervantes introduction, c. 1872-80 to de la Rada).
